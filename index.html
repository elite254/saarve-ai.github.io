How it works

From pilot to production in four steps

Most teams start with a scoped pilot on a real batch of data, then scale the same workforce into ongoing production.

01

Scope & pilot

We review your guidelines, data and edge cases, then run a small paid pilot so you can validate quality before committing.

02

Team assembly & training

A workforce is selected and trained specifically on your task, calibrated against your gold-standard examples.

03

Production & QA loops

Work runs inside your existing tools or ours, with layered QA, agreement scoring and daily throughput reporting.

04

Reporting & iteration

Your program manager reviews guideline drift and edge cases with you weekly, refining instructions as your model evolves.
